Help & Documentation

Everything you need to know about using SignAI

How to Use

  1. Navigate to Live Detection page
  2. Click "Open Camera" to start your webcam
  3. Position yourself in front of the camera with good lighting
  4. Perform ASL signs — detections appear in real-time
  5. Review the Sentence Builder for accumulated text
  6. Click "Generate Meaningful Interpretation" for AI-powered output
  7. Use "Play Audio" to hear the generated sentence
  8. Press Q or ESC to stop the camera

Camera & Lighting Tips

  • Use a well-lit room with even lighting
  • Avoid backlighting (don't sit with a window behind you)
  • Keep your hands within the camera frame
  • Use a plain background for best results
  • Position camera at face/chest level
  • Ensure your face is visible for emotion detection
  • Avoid fast movements — hold signs briefly

Privacy & Data Usage

No Video Storage

Video streams are processed in real-time and never stored on any server.

Local Processing

All detection happens locally. Session data is stored only in your browser.

Your Data, Your Control

You can delete all session history at any time from the Transcript page.
